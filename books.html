Books

A few books I've read.

  • The Dark Forest — Liu Cixin
    Science Fiction · ★★★★½

    The Dark Forest by Liu Cixin is a science fiction novel that explores humanity's struggle to survive an impending alien invasion, delving into complex strategies, the concept of cosmic sociology, and the chilling idea that the universe is a dangerous place where civilizations must hide to survive.

  • 1984 — George Orwell
    Classics · ★★★★☆

    1984 by George Orwell is a dystopian novel that depicts a totalitarian regime where the government, led by Big Brother, controls every aspect of life, including thought, through surveillance, propaganda, and fear, ultimately exploring the dangers of absolute power and the loss of individual freedom.

  • Colourless Tsukuru Tazaki and His Years of Pilgrimage — Haruki Murakami
    Romance · ★★★★☆

    Colorless Tsukuru Tazaki and His Years of Pilgrimage by Haruki Murakami follows a man grappling with the emotional aftermath of being suddenly cut off from his close-knit group of friends, prompting a journey of self-discovery and reconciliation with his past

  • Brave New World — Aldous Huxley
    Classics · ★★★½☆

    Brave New World by Aldous Huxley is a dystopian novel that portrays a future society governed by technological control, genetic engineering, and societal conditioning, where individuality and free will are sacrificed for stability and happiness.

  • 3 Body Problem — Liu Cixin
    Science Fiction · ★★½☆☆

    The Three-Body Problem by Liu Cixin is a science fiction novel that explores humanity's first contact with an alien civilization, blending scientific concepts with philosophical questions about survival and the future of civilization.
